Synopsis

Ananya dreams of a man she has never met. In the dream a Goddess tells her to marry him. His name is Shaktiyuth. She wakes up sure of the name and of nothing else, and slowly, without ever having seen his face, she begins to fall in love.

Years earlier, in a house among trees, a boy was born with an intellectual disability that doctors said might never lift. His parents, busy running a company together, hired a caretaker named Kleeba. The boy could not say her name properly. He said "Kleem" instead, and went on saying it, over and over, for years. Nothing changed. Then, one night, something did.

Ananya's friend tells her to forget the dream. Her father has already promised her to the son of a powerful family, and he seems to know more about Shaktiyuth than he is willing to say. The Goddess, it seems, has not finished.

Main Characters

Ananya (Anu)

A college student who cannot explain her certainty and has stopped trying to. Gentle, stubborn, and braver than the people around her expect.

Shaktiyuth (Shakti)

The boy who spent his childhood saying one word, and the man he grows into. He says less than he knows.

Kleeba

His caretaker, who has no idea what the word she is being called is doing. She only knows the boy she looks after.

Sanya

Ananya's closest friend and her voice of reason. She scolds, she worries, and she is honest enough to admit when she was wrong.

Genres & Themes

Genres: Devotional Fantasy, Romance, Family Drama, Thriller

Themes: Faith and Destiny, Devotion, Quiet Transformation, Love Without Sight, Family and Betrayal
